# Concept-centric cases

Outcomes are as reported by recap sites, Wikipedia and press. Revenue and user figures after the show are mostly self-reported.

| Company | Concept | Pitch numbers | Objections | Deal and outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bundil (US S10) | Invest spare change in crypto | 2 months old, about $2.70 acquisition cost per user; asked $100k for 10% | Greiner: skeptical of crypto; Cuban: conflict with a competitor; Daymond: too early | O'Leary, $100k for 50%, accepted on air. Died in diligence over dilution protection. Shut down 2023 |
| Goalsetter (US S10) | Kids' savings and gifting | $40k sales, $21.1M previously raised; asked $200k for 4% ($5M) | Greiner: not investable; Cuban: complicated; others passed | O'Leary offered 25%; founder countered 8%; no deal. $15M Series A Dec 2021 (TechCrunch), pivot to B2B; $9.6M extension 2024 |
| Spare (US S10) | Virtual ATM app | $347k raised on notes; asked $500k for 3.5% (about $14.3M) | Sharks questioned logistics and whether merchants would hold cash | Cuban, $500k for 12% plus 2% advisory, accepted. Never closed; dead by Jul 2019 |
| ReThink (US S8) | Warn before sending abusive messages | No revenue; 1,500-teen trial; asked $100k for 20% | Corcoran and O'Leary: more charity than business; Herjavec: same as parental controls | Daymond offered 30%; Greiner and Cuban offered original terms. Closing not confirmed. $300k foundation grant 2020 |
| #besomebody (US S8) | Booking app for experiences with experts | $2M prior seed, 5M hashtag followers; asked $1M for 10% ($10M) | Liability, exclusivity, abrasive founder, another app that won't make it | No deal. Sold 2017, shut 2018; founder pivoted to corporate training |
| Candi (US S13) | Celebrity video calls | $100k sales; asked $500k for 15% | Valuation; too early; talent loses interest | No deal. Still operating |
| Bot-It (US S15) | Bots for ordinary people | About $30k in sales, 350 subscribers; asked $150k for 10% | O'Leary: ethics; Rubin: anti-bot safeguards; Corcoran and Greiner: not their space | Cuban and Rubin, $300k for 30%. Closing disputed, probably not |
| Overplay (US S15) | User-created games | 150k downloads, no revenue; asked $500k for 2.5% ($20M) | Prior losses on similar apps | Cuban, 4% plus a paid promoter role. Closed |

## Judgement from the sample (small, recap sources)

- Valuation against proof was the top objection. Asks of $10M to $20M got no offer or an equity concession.
- Concept pitches needed a wedge with a number. Where the wedge was thin, sharks said "too early".
- Deals on air often did not close on a concept pitch (Spare, Bundil).
- Companies with an unmoved floor (Goalsetter, Swimply) raised more elsewhere.
- Ethics and regulation objections are not answered by tone; they need a mechanism.
