# Service-centric cases

Outcomes are as reported by recap sites, Wikipedia and press. "Unconfirmed" means no independent source.

| Company | Service | Pitch numbers | Objections | Deal and outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HelloPrenup (US S13) | $599 online prenup against about $5,000 with a lawyer | $20k lifetime sales; projected $4.1M next year; asked $150k for 10% ($1.5M) | O'Leary: cannot value without CAC; Greiner: too early; Herjavec: those who need one use a lawyer | O'Leary and Nirav Tolia, $150k for 30%. Closed Dec 2021. $3M+ sales reported by 2023-24 (self-reported) |
| Hidrent (US S13) | Off-duty firefighters hired by homeowners | $850k lifetime sales, 2021 YTD $325k; asked $300k for 8% | Tolia: local businesses are hard to scale; Cuban: depends on digital ads; O'Leary: a long slog | Greiner and Herjavec, 33.3%. Closing unconfirmed. Later reported $662k seed (self-reported) |
| Wedfuly (US S13) | Virtual wedding planning | $700k 2020 sales, about $1M by 2021 with $300k profit; asked $200k for 5% ($4M) | Daymond: people want traditional weddings; Cuban: cannot scale | Herjavec, 10%. Did not close. Acquired by Wedgewood Weddings Aug 2023, price undisclosed |
| Hotels by Day (US S8) | Day-use hotel bookings | 6,500 bookings, $127k revenue; asked $750k for 10% | Cuban: numbers too small; Herjavec: chains will copy it; O'Leary: why don't existing sites do this | No deal. About 300 partner hotels at pitch, 450 by 2018 (reported) |
| Swimply (US 2020) | Peer-to-peer pool rental | $215k revenue, $42k net profit, 25% take rate on the show per one recap; 20% host plus 10% guest fee per Wikipedia (sources differ); asked $300k for 5% ($6M) | O'Leary: valuation; Cuban: founder self-awareness; Corcoran: did not understand it | No offers. $10M Series A and $40M round in 2021 |
| Woof (US S14) | Wellness-check concierge | 300+ users, 100+ paid; asked $100k for 10% | Daymond: name and copyright; Herjavec: audience too small | No deal; later outcome not stated |
| ESAI (US S16) | AI college admissions coaching in place of $750k counselling packages | About $70k sales, $49.99 a month premium; asked $250k for 5% | O'Leary: why not ChatGPT; Corcoran: retention; Greiner: prior raise too large | Cuban, $250k for 5% plus advisory shares; closing unconfirmed |
| Brightwheel (US 2016) | Childcare management app, parents are users and schools pay | Asked $400k for 4% | Not recorded | Cuban and Sacca, $600k for about 6.67%. $55M Series C Feb 2021 at a reported $600M valuation |
| Sprive (UK 2026) | Mortgage overpayments funded by cashback and remortgage commission | 25,000 users, £20 CAC, losing £30k to £35k a month | Neville and Bartlett: valuation | Three Dragons invested £50k for 5%. Series A reported Sept 2026 |
| StoryTerrace (UK S17) | Online biography service | Asked £90k for 5% | Not recorded | Failed on the show; in administration 2023 |
| Look After My Bills (UK S16) | Bill comparison | £120k for 3% | Not recorded | Signed; sold 2019 |
| Snapsmile (Canada S18) | Free dental check-up app for clinics | 5,000+ images; 20+ partner clinics growing to about 30 | Business model changed | Romanow offered $75K for 20%; deal fell through on the model change |

## What the cases suggest (judgement, small sample)

- The services that grew had repeat real-world demand or a recurring base.
- Price-anchor pitches against a named alternative (HelloPrenup, Swimply) landed most clearly.
- Objections cluster on scale, copyability by a chain or professional, and acquisition cost.
- A deal on air did not decide the outcome: Swimply and Wedfuly grew without a closed shark deal.
