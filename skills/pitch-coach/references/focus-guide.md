# Focus guide (use when the focus skills are not installed)

Condensed from the four focus skills. Cases are as reported by recap sites, Wikipedia and press; post-show figures are often self-reported.

## Founder-centric

Shape: one-line who and what; why this founder (one concrete proof); the problem as you met it; traction relative to time worked; the weakness, said before they ask; the ask sized to proof; what you want from this investor.

Likeliest questions: Why you? What have you built before? Who else is on the team? What could go wrong? What will you do if we say no?

Cases: Sprive's founder stated losses (£30k to £35k a month) and CAC (£20) plainly and three Dragons invested. M14's founder was called "one of the most appealing individuals" and drew five offers; the deal died when the valuation moved. Hati's founder had no prior business and £48 of revenue; Bartlett invested £150k for 30% against a 5% ask. Swimply's founder lost offers on self-awareness and talking too fast, then the company raised $10M and $40M.

## Service-centric

Shape: the job and the price gap; how a job runs; unit economics for one job (price, cost to deliver, acquisition cost); proof people come back; how it scales; why an incumbent does not simply do this; the ask.

Likeliest questions: What does it cost to get a customer? How do you scale beyond one city or provider type? Why can't a chain or a lawyer do this? How much is repeat?

Cases: HelloPrenup priced $599 against about $5,000 and closed with O'Leary and Nirav Tolia for 30%. Hidrent (off-duty firefighters, $850k lifetime sales) drew a 33.3% offer. Wedfuly took 10% from Herjavec and was later acquired by Wedgewood Weddings. Hotels by Day was told chains would copy it and grew anyway.

## Product-centric

Shape: demo or hook in 20 seconds; one-liner; traction in paying terms (paid versus free, with the denominator named); price anchor; CAC and channel; why it cannot be copied; the ask with the multiple spoken.

Likeliest questions: Why this valuation? What are your sales? CAC and scale? Why can't a bigger player copy it? Retention? For AI products: why not ChatGPT?

Cases: Hopscotch had 6,200 paying subscribers out of about 200k monthly users and took a lower valuation to close. Overplay had 150k downloads and no revenue, offered a paid promoter role, and closed at 4%. Autio asked $20M on 500k downloads and got no deal. Bot-It claimed its bots were "undetectable" without explaining and three sharks passed.

## Concept-centric

Shape: the change in one sentence; why now; the first wedge with a number; proof of pull; the objection answered first, with a mechanism; the valuation line; the ask and the milestone it proves.

Likeliest questions: Is it a business or a cause? Why now? Who is the first customer? Ethics or regulation? Why this valuation?

Cases: Bundil took O'Leary's 50% on air, the deal died in diligence and the company shut in 2023. Goalsetter refused 25%, raised a $15M Series A in December 2021 and pivoted to B2B. Spare asked about a $14.3M valuation and the sharks gasped; the deal never closed. ReThink had no revenue and was told it was "more charity than business".
