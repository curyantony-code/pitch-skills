Outcomes are as reported by recap sites, Wikipedia and press. Revenue and user figures after the show are mostly self-reported.

Closing unclear. $5M annual revenue Oct 2023 (self-reported) |

## Judgement from the sample (small, recap sources)

- Paid conversion and price made the strongest product pitches (Hopscotch).
- Download counts alone got pushback (Autio, Beulr).
- Deals closed more often at small cheques and simple terms.
- AI products draw a direct substitute question: ESAI's "why not ChatGPT".
- Unexplained technical claims cost offers (Bot-It).
