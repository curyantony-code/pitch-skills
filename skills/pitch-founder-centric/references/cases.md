# Founder-centric cases

Sources are recap sites, Wikipedia tables and press. Outcomes are as reported; "unconfirmed" means no independent source found.

## Founder helped

| Founder or company | What the founder did | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Sprive (Dragons' Den UK, 2026) | Stated losses (£30k to £35k a month) and CAC (£20) plainly | Meaden, Suleyman and Jones invested £50k for 5%; Gary Neville and Bartlett dropped out over valuation. Series A reported Sept 2026, self-reported growth |
| M14 Industries (UK, S14) | Jones called the founder "one of the most appealing individuals"; 150,000 users but about £1k a month turnover | All five Dragons offered; agreed £80k for 20% on air; deal collapsed after a larger client proposed a £1.5m pre-money valuation. Company dissolved 2024 |
| Hopscotch (US, S12) | Took Cuban's lower valuation ($10M ask to about $5M) | Deal reported on air; closing unclear |
| Brightwheel (US, 2016) | Ran a bidding war between Cuban and Sacca | $600k for about 6.67%; later raised $55M Series C at a reported $600M valuation |
| Swimply, Goalsetter, TickX | Walked from offers they thought too low | Each raised more elsewhere (Swimply $10M and $40M in 2021, Goalsetter $15M Series A, TickX £750k) |
| Zapier (YC) | Rejected on the first application, kept building alongside jobs | $1.2M to $1.3M seed, 2012 |
| Instacart (YC) | Delivered a six-pack through the app to a YC partner | Admitted to S12 |
| Airbnb (YC W09) | Showed three weeks of fees and a hand-run profit target | YC put in $20,000 |

## Founder hurt

| Founder or company | What happened |
|---|---|
| Hati (UK, S23) | Jones objected to the £3m valuation, little traction and no prior successful business. £48 revenue, 5,000 downloads. Bartlett invested £150k for 30% against a 5% ask |
| Swimply (US, 2020) | Cuban: founder lacked self-awareness. Herjavec: talked too fast. No offers. Company later raised $10M and $40M |
| Trippie (US, S9) | 179 users; Herjavec and another shark bristled at the founder's "no rich uncle" remark. No deal |
| #besomebody (US, S8) | $1M for 10%; sharks cited an abrasive founder among the reasons. Sold 2017, shut 2018 |
| Bot-It (US, S15) | Claimed the bots were "undetectable", did not explain. O'Leary, Corcoran and Greiner passed. Deal with Cuban and Rubin unconfirmed as closed |
| ESAI (US, S16) | Personalised demo opened strong; answer to "why not ChatGPT" was thin. Cuban offered; deal closing unconfirmed |

## YC reading of founders (from Graham, Seibel and Livingston)

- The most important application question is something impressive each founder has built or achieved.
- Interviews are about 10 minutes of questions; candor is rewarded and bluffing penalised.
- Traction is what you accomplished relative to time worked.
- Disclose known flaws.
- Not verified: Notion, Duolingo, Cluely, and any 2024 to 2026 YC consumer app.
