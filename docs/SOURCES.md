# Sources

The cases in the skills were assembled from the pages below by research agents in November 2026. Most are recap blogs, Wikipedia tables and press articles, not transcripts. Some pages were only seen as search results and could not be fully opened. Figures reported after a show are often self-reported by the company or by an aggregator; the skills tell Claude to say "reported" for those. Where two sources disagreed, the skills say so. Nothing here is a verbatim transcript.

This project is not affiliated with Shark Tank, Dragons' Den or Y Combinator. Show and company names belong to their owners.
