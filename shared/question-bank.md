# Question bank: what investors ask software and service founders

Built from about 70 software, app and platform pitches on Shark Tank (US, Australia, India), Dragons' Den (UK, Canada) and Y Combinator material. Rankings are a judgement from a small sample, mostly recap sites and Wikipedia tables, with no verbatim transcripts. Treat them as likely, not guaranteed.

## The eight questions, in the order they usually come

| # | Question | What the investor is really testing | What a strong answer contains | Seen in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why is this worth that valuation? | Whether the ask matches the proof | The ask divided by revenue or paying users, said out loud, then the reason it is still fair (growth rate, margin, prior raise) | Swimply, Candi, Overplay, Card.io, Goalsetter, Spare, Hopscotch, Hati, Sprive, Cattleytics |
| 2 | What are your sales, or why are there none? | Whether anyone pays | Paying customers and monthly revenue first, downloads second. If there is no revenue, the proof of demand that replaces it (trial, waitlist, retention) | Left Field, Parrot Finance, Hello Prenup, Overplay, Candi, M14 |
| 3 | What does it cost to get a customer, and how do you scale? | Whether growth is affordable | CAC in one sentence, the channel it comes from, what happens to it at 10x | Hello Prenup, Bundil, Hidrent, Hopscotch, Woof, Tabby, Sprive |
| 4 | Why can't a bigger player copy this? | Moat | A specific barrier: data, network, regulation, speed plus a distribution edge. Not "we are first" | Hotels by Day, ReThink, Parrot Finance, ESAI (ChatGPT), Bot-It (detection), Hati |
| 5 | Who else has put money in, and on what terms? | Cap table and signal | The amount, the valuation, who holds what, any SAFE or debt | Goalsetter, Overplay, Parrot Finance, ESAI |
| 6 | Why you? | Whether this founder finishes | One concrete thing you built or did that relates, and your honest weakness | Hati, M14, Beulr, Trippie, Swimply, #besomebody |
| 7 | Will they stay? | Retention and churn | Cohort retention or repeat rate, or a plain statement that you do not have it yet and the date you will | ESAI (Corcoran), M14 (churn in dating apps) |
| 8 | How much do you need, and what happens to the other investors' share? | Capital need and dilution | Use of funds with a milestone, and the round it leads to | AppyParking, Hopscotch, Spare |

YC adds two that the TV shows rarely ask: "Who are your first users and how do you know they want it?" and "What could go wrong, specifically?" Partners also interrupt the answer, so pause before replying.

## Answer rules that held across cases

- Lead with the number, then the context. Sprive's founder said the loss (£30k to £35k a month) and the CAC (£20) in one breath, and three Dragons invested.
- Never wave away the hardest question. Bot-It said its bots were "undetectable" and did not explain; three sharks passed. ESAI's ChatGPT answer was thin, and Herjavec and Greiner went out.
- A claim with no number or mechanism behind it counts against you.
- Founders who were abrasive, talked too fast or lacked self-awareness lost offers they could otherwise have had (Swimply, Trippie, #besomebody).

## When an offer comes

- Expect more equity than you asked for. Bot-It asked 10% and gave 30% across two sharks. Hati asked 5% and gave 30%.
- Combining two investors is allowed. Bot-It's founders asked Cuban and Rubin to join and they did.
- Trade value instead of equity where you can. Overplay offered a paid promoter role and settled at 4%.
- Know your floor before you walk in. Hopscotch cut its valuation from $10M to $5M and closed. Swimply, Goalsetter and TickX walked from low offers and raised more elsewhere.
- An on-air handshake is the start of diligence. Spare, Bundil, Wedfuly, Hopscotch, Tabby, Pouch, M14 and Choppity did not close or are unconfirmed. Small cheques ($150k to $500k) with simple terms closed most often: Hello Prenup, Overplay, Card.io.
- Closing is not surviving. Signed UK deals such as ParkingPerx, Cheesegeek and Luxe Collective later folded.
